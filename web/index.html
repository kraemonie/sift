One matrix. Fewer building blocks.

SVD writes a matrix as A = UΣVᵀ. The singular values in Σ order its building blocks by strength. Keeping the first k gives Aₖ, the best rank-k approximation in squared matrix error.

Uk×Σk×Vkᵀ=Ak

Numerical rank counts independent directions above a tolerance. Retained rank is your choice. RGB images have one matrix per color channel; audio samples are arranged in 128-sample rows. Energy retention measures squared singular values—not perceptual quality.

SIFT / FOR THE FRAMES & THE FREQUENCIES.SVD · Low-rank approximation · Offline

A little less. Still you.

Sift is an offline photo and sound studio for exploring SVD and low-rank approximation. Keep fewer building blocks and compare what changes.

Your files stay on your device. Imported photos keep their original resolution in the reference and initial preview. Rank adjustments use a working copy up to 192px on the longest side; the reconstructed PNG uses that working resolution. Audio uses up to eight seconds at 16 kHz mono.

Factor archives store the mathematical building blocks. Exported PNG and WAV files may be larger than the source JPEG or MP3. Energy retained measures matrix information, not perceived quality.
